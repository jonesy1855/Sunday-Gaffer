# Privacy Policy for Sunday Gaffer

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Sunday Gaffer** ("the App") handles user information.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
**Sunday Gaffer does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.** 

All game progress, club names, transfer records, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's internal storage (`SharedPreferences` / local database). If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, this data is permanently deleted from your device.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Analytics
The App operates completely offline and does not integrate third-party advertising networks, user tracking SDKs, or external analytics services.

## 3. Children’s Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal information from any users, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global regulations.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
If future updates introduce online features, leaderboards, or cloud saves, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly prior to release.
